If you were one of the 8.8 million federal student loan borrowers currently in default, then the New Year didn’t start off great. In early January, the White House announced that it would officially begin garnishing wages, tax refunds, and other benefits from defaulted federal student loan borrowers beginning the week of Jan. 7.
